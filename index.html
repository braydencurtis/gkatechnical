How It Works

From Call to Fixed — Fast

A streamlined process designed to get your kitchen back to full operation as quickly as possible.

01
Call or Submit a Request
Call us directly at (406) 403-4894 or use the online form. Describe the issue and we'll get your technician scheduled.
02
On-Site Diagnosis
Our technician arrives with tools and common parts, performs a full diagnostic, and provides a verbal estimate before starting any work (written estimate provided upon request).
03
Repair & Testing
Once approved, we complete the repair and thoroughly test the equipment to ensure it's running to factory specifications.
04
Follow-Up & Warranty
We follow up after 48 hours to confirm everything is still running properly. Your repair is covered by our 90-day labor warranty.
